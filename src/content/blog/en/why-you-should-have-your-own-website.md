You should have your own website because it is the only place on the web where you control the content, the design and how you present yourself — without depending on algorithms, social networks or third-party platforms.

## The problem with platforms

Social networks change their rules, reach limits and, often, the algorithms decide who sees what you publish. A website of your own is your permanent space: the domain is yours, the content is yours and the presentation is yours.

## What a personal website gives you

- **Full control**: you decide layout, typography, structure and even how to optimize for search engines.
- **Longevity**: as long as the domain is paid for, the content stays — years later, the link still works.
- **Traffic ownership**: visitors come straight to you, with no middlemen.
- **Living portfolio**: the best showcase for anyone working in technology or creation.

## How to start

You don't need complexity. A static blog with few images and quality writing — exactly like this site — is enough. Tools like Astro keep the maintenance cost minimal.

> Your website is not a weekend project; it is an asset that appreciates over time.

## Conclusion

The web is open and still rewards those with their own presence. If you don't have a site yet, this is the moment: start small, publish something honest and let time work in your favor.
